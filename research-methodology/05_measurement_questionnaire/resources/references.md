# منابع علمی ماژول ۵

منابع زیر راهنمای مطالعهٔ تکمیلی‌اند. وجود یک مقاله یا استاندارد در فهرست به معنای اعتبارسنجی ابزار یا اجرای چک‌لیست در این فاز نیست. عنوان و شناسه‌ها در فهرست ماشینی ثبت شده‌اند؛ دامنهٔ بررسی هر منبع نیز در همان‌جا آمده است.

## M05-L01: سازه و سنجه

- DeVellis RF, Thorpe CT. Scale Development: Theory and Applications. [صفحهٔ ناشر](https://us.sagepub.com/en-us/nam/scale-development/book269156)
- AERA, APA, NCME. Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing. [وبگاه رسمی استانداردها](https://www.testingstandards.net/)
- Mokkink LB, et al. The COSMIN study reached international consensus on taxonomy, terminology, and definitions of measurement properties for health-related patient-reported outcomes. [Journal of Clinical Epidemiology, 2010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclinepi.2010.02.006)

## M05-L02: ساخت و سازگارکردن پرسش‌نامه

- Beaton DE, et al. Guidelines for the process of cross-cultural adaptation of self-report measures. [Spine, 2000](https://doi.org/10.1097/00007632-200012150-00014)
- Willis GB. Cognitive Interviewing: A Tool for Improving Questionnaire Design. [راهنمای NCBI Bookshelf](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK144065/)
- Wild D, et al. Principles of good practice for the translation and cultural adaptation process for patient-reported outcomes measures. [Value in Health, 2005](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1524-4733.2005.04054.x)
- DeVellis RF, Thorpe CT. Scale Development: Theory and Applications. [صفحهٔ ناشر](https://us.sagepub.com/en-us/nam/scale-development/book269156)

## M05-L03: روایی و پایایی

- Mokkink LB, et al. The COSMIN study reached international consensus on taxonomy, terminology, and definitions of measurement properties for health-related patient-reported outcomes. [Journal of Clinical Epidemiology, 2010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclinepi.2010.02.006)
- Koo TK, Li MY. A guideline of selecting and reporting intraclass correlation coefficients for reliability research. [Journal of Chiropractic Medicine, 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcm.2016.02.012)
- Terwee CB, et al. COSMIN methodology for evaluating the content validity of patient-reported outcome measures. [Quality of Life Research, 2018](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11136-018-1829-0)
- Bland JM, Altman DG. Statistical methods for assessing agreement between two methods of clinical measurement. [The Lancet, 1986](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(86)90837-8)

- Lord SJ, et al. SuperPlots: Communicating reproducibility and variability in cell biology. [Journal of Cell Biology, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1083/jcb.202001064)
